## README and Open Graph images

When the landing page logo, headline, or intro changes, render the updated header from `website/index.html` and include these images in the same change:

- `website/hero-light.png` and `website/hero-dark.png` are the light and dark README banners. Capture the wordmark and intro in each theme, with enough space on the left for the lightning icon.
- `website/og.png` is the 1280×640 dark social preview. Keep the site and repository URLs in the footer.

Open all three PNGs before publishing and check that the lightning icon, text, and OG footer fit without clipping.
